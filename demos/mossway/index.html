Our story

It started with one soggy backyard.

In 2018 our neighbor's yard in Plaza Midwood flooded every time it rained. Instead of more drainage pipe, we dug a rain garden and filled it with native plants. By summer it was full of butterflies, and three more neighbors asked for one.

Now we're a crew of six, and most of our work still comes from neighbors looking over the fence.
