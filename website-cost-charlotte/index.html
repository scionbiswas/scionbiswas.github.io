How much a website costs per month to keep running

After launch, a small business website has two running costs you can't skip, and one you can:

  • Domain name, about $11 to $19 a year: the yourbusiness.com address. A .com renews at about $11 a year at cost-price registrars like Cloudflare and closer to $19 at others.
  • Hosting, free to a few dollars a month: I hand-build sites as plain, fast files, so they don't need a paid page-builder plan to stay online.
  • Upkeep, optional: you can make changes yourself, or pick a care plan after launch.
  • Keep-up, $25 a month: small edits whenever you need them, like new hours, prices or photos, and more.
  • Account and server, $60 a month: everything in Keep-up, and more.

For comparison, a Squarespace site costs $19 to $99 a month and Wix $17 to $159 a month (billed yearly, 2026 prices), and the site stops if you stop paying.
