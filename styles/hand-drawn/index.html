What the hand-drawn style is

Hand-drawn adds sketched touches, like marker rings, underlines and badges, on top of a bold color. It makes the site feel like your menu boards and recipe cards rather than a template.

It works best for places with a story: a family diner, a business that has been on the same corner for decades. There is room for a timeline, old photos and a few words from the owner.
