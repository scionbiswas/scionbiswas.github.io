Our story

A South Boulevard name, twice over.

Skyland began as Pete Politis's family restaurant in 1960 and came back in 1994 under Jimmy Kakavitsas, who learned the business working for him. It has been a Charlotte breakfast habit ever since.

  • 1960Pete Politis opens the first Skyland on South Boulevard, near Woodlawn Road. Pizza, fried seafood, fried chicken and home-style plates.
  • 1975The original closes after about 15 years.
  • 1994Jimmy Kakavitsas, who had worked for Politis for years, reopens Skyland half a mile away in the old Plantation Family Restaurant, with Politis helping. The menu looks a lot like the first one.
  • TodayStill on South Boulevard, just before Woodlawn, serving breakfast all day.
